# 3kW Electric Go-Kart

A converted electric go-kart powered by a 3kW motor and [72V battery pack I built from recycled 18650 cells](../gokart-battery-pack/README.md). The kart itself was mostly an assembly project — the build, the controller saga, and the frame repairs below.

![](media/kart-frame-and-motor.mp4)

## Components

- **$50 3kW motor controller kit** from AliExpress
- **$100 3kW 72V motor** from AliExpress
- **$200 second-hand frame** bought from a schoolmate

## The controller

The controller took by far the longest to figure out. There was a mystery "activation code" type thing I had to do, where specific buttons and wires had to be pressed in a certain order for it to actually run. The manual was written so badly that I almost gave up entirely and assumed I'd been scammed on the motor controller. It was very frustrating — but I got it working the next day, and I'm still not sure what I actually fixed.

## The battery box

I would have preferred a fibreglass battery box, but I didn't have any and it would have been expensive, so with those financial limitations I settled on a wooden box. To stop the battery from sliding around inside, I added yoga mat padding and spacing to apply constant pressure. I also hooked up my phone to the pack over Bluetooth, so I could shut the battery off whenever I wanted — including if it was being ridden by someone I didn't want riding it.

## The frame

The frame was a disaster. It was badly made to begin with: the bearings kept falling out, and the brakes did not work at all. I had to spend a large amount of time fixing the brake line and brake pad placement, and dealing with the bearing issues.

It also sat completely stanced. Because the kart was lowered that far, you had to wrestle the wheel to turn — turning effectively lifted the cart up and down on its suspension.
